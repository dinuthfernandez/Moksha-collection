MOKSHA COLLECTIONS - WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES BEHIND THE SCENES
===========================================================
A plain-language list of the automatic features working in the background
of your website. You don't have to do anything for these to work.


A. PRODUCTS AND STOCK (connected to Zoho Inventory)
---------------------------------------------------
1. Automatic product import from Zoho
   Products you add in Zoho Inventory appear on the website by themselves:
   name, price, photos, category, size, color and stock. No double typing.

2. Automatic stock updates
   When stock changes in Zoho (for example a sale in your physical shop),
   the website stock is updated automatically. Customers always see the
   real quantity available.

3. Shop sales reduce website stock instantly
   When something is sold offline in your shop, Zoho tells the website and
   the stock goes down, so you never sell an item twice.

4. Exact size and color stock
   Every size and color combination (for example "3XL - Mix Colour") has its
   own stock. When one is ordered, only that exact item is reduced, never
   the other sizes or colors.

5. Size and color shown together
   Customers choose "Size - Color" in one place. When they change it, the
   available stock changes to match.

6. Similar designs grouped into one product
   The same design in different sizes and colors shows as one product card
   on the shop pages instead of many repeated items.

7. Protection against accidental hiding of products
   If a wrong or empty update ever comes from Zoho, the system refuses to
   hide all your products by mistake.

8. Automatic stock return on cancel or return
   If an order is cancelled (by the customer, admin or delivery rider) or a
   return is completed, the items go back into stock immediately, on the
   website and in Zoho.

9. Hidden internal product codes
   Internal codes (like MC-xxxx) are used only by the system to find the
   exact item. Customers never see them.


B. ORDERS AND INVOICES
----------------------
10. Automatic Zoho invoice for every order
    Each website order creates an invoice in Zoho Inventory, so your
    accounting stays complete without manual work.

11. Automatic invoice cancel (void)
    If an order is cancelled or returned, its Zoho invoice is marked as void
    automatically. (Already-paid invoices may need a manual credit note.)

12. Order emails to customers
    Customers get an email when their order is placed and when it is
    completed, sent from your company email.

13. Order history that stays correct
    Past orders always show the right size, color and item details.

14. Return requests
    Customers can request a return from their order. The request can only be
    made once, and the admin can approve and complete it with one click.


C. CART AND CHECKOUT
--------------------
15. Stock held while items are in the cart
    When a customer adds an item to the cart, that quantity is reserved for
    a short time so someone else cannot take it before they pay.

16. Cart and checkout only for logged-in customers
    Customers must log in to add items and place orders, which keeps orders
    tied to real accounts and prevents fake or spam orders.

17. Discount coupons
    Create coupon codes with rules; the system checks and applies them at
    checkout automatically.

18. Delivery charges and free delivery
    Delivery cost is calculated automatically, with free delivery once the
    order reaches the amount you set.

19. Delivery time estimates
    Customers see an estimated delivery date range.


D. CUSTOMERS AND SECURITY
-------------------------
20. Customer accounts
    Sign up, login, profile with phone number and country code, saved
    addresses.

21. Wishlist
    Customers can save favourite products and come back to them later.

22. Product reviews
    Customers can leave reviews on products.

23. Welcome email
    New customers automatically receive a branded welcome email.

24. Forgot password by email code
    Customers get a 6-digit code by email to reset their password.

25. Password reset lock
    After 5 wrong reset codes the account is locked for 1 hour, to stop
    anyone trying to guess codes.

26. Secure admin panel
    The admin panel is at a private web address, protected by a login, and
    all admin actions require that login.

27. Customer ban
    Admin can ban a customer account if needed.


E. ADMIN PANEL
--------------
28. Dashboard
    Sales and order statistics at a glance.

29. Order management
    View all orders, update status, cancel, and see exact size and color of
    each item.

30. Returns page
    See all return requests and complete them (stock is restored for you).

31. Email campaigns
    Send marketing emails to all customers, choose to send from info@ or
    sales@, add a button link, and send a test email to yourself first. The
    email uses your brand design and greets each customer by name.

32. Coupons and delivery settings
    Manage discount codes, delivery rates and free-delivery limits.


F. DELIVERY RIDER
-----------------
33. Delivery rider page
    Riders have their own page to see assigned orders and update delivery
    status (delivered, cancelled, etc.).

34. Rider cancel restocks automatically
    If a rider marks an order as cancelled, stock and invoice are handled
    automatically.


G. SPEED AND RELIABILITY
------------------------
35. Fast product loading
    Products and categories are cached so pages open quickly, with a branded
    Moksha loading screen while everything loads.

36. Automatic retry on connection problems
    If the database connection hiccups, the system retries by itself so
    pages do not hang.

37. Website analytics
    Visits and activity are recorded so you can see how customers use the
    site.

38. Automatic safety checks
    Failed emails or Zoho calls are recorded in logs and never stop an
    order from going through.
